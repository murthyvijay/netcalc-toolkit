# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Optics: ITU-T G.694.1 DWDM grid and flexgrid slots, optical power budget and
  reach, chromatic dispersion and PMD limits, coherent line rates, EDFA planning.
- Wireless: free-space path loss, EIRP, Fresnel clearance and radio horizon,
  Shannon capacity, noise floor and SINR, 802.11n/ac/ax PHY rates and channels.
- Queueing: Little's law and buffer occupancy, Erlang B and C sizing,
  Pollaczek-Khinchine and Kingman waits, M/M/1/K loss and buffer sizing.
- Traffic management: DSCP/ToS/CoS mapping with RFC 4594 classes, RFC 2697 and
  RFC 2698 colour markers, RED/WRED, max-min fair and DWRR schedulers.
- Routing: BGP best-path selection, iBGP mesh vs route reflectors and ASN
  formats, Dijkstra with ECMP and loop-free alternates, IS-IS NETs and metrics,
  BFD timers, VRRP/HSRP, MPLS and SR label math, EVPN scale, convergence budgets.

## 1.0.0

- Layer 1: BER confidence and test time, Q-factor, Reed-Solomon FEC (KR4/KP4)
  post-FEC BER, threshold, margin and coding gain, PAM/QAM SER, OSNR.
- Performance: latency budget, RFC 3550 jitter, loss and burst analysis,
  BDP and window limits, Mathis and Padhye TCP models.
- CCNA/CCNP: subnetting, VLSM, wildcard masks, summarization, OSPF cost,
  EIGRP metric and feasibility, STP timers and costs, QoS queueing.
- Protocols: header overhead and goodput for Ethernet/IP/TCP/UDP/VXLAN/RoCEv2,
  MTU/MSS and encapsulation headroom.
- AI fabric: ECMP vs spraying imbalance, go-back-N vs selective retransmit,
  multipath reliable connection sizing, PFC headroom, ECN, DCQCN, buffer
  sizing and collective bandwidth.
- `netcalc` command, examples and GitHub Actions CI.
